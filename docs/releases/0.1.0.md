The first release: a dashboard for Skyward Family Access (Qmlativ) that shows your student's
grades, missing assignments and what's coming up.

## What's in it

- **Grades for every class and grading period**, with the percentage and the breakdown by
  category (homework, quizzes, tests, ...), and a trend over time as grades change.
- **Missing assignments** for the whole year, and **upcoming assignments** with their due dates.
- **A changes feed:** new scores, grade changes, work that goes missing and work that's turned in.
- **Scheduled updates.** The app signs in to Skyward on a schedule you choose
  (`SKYWARD_SYNC_CRON`, every 3 hours from 6am to 9pm by default) and keeps what it fetches on
  your machine. The dashboard never waits on Skyward.
- Light and dark mode, and a layout that works on a phone.

## Installing

Create a folder with a `.env` file holding your Skyward address and login (see
[`.env.example`](../../.env.example)), download
[`docker-compose.yml`](../../docker-compose.yml) into it, then:

```bash
docker compose up -d
```

Open `http://<your server>:8080`. The first update runs right away and takes about 30 seconds.

Skyward allows one sign-in per account at a time, so each update may sign you out of Skyward in
your browser.
